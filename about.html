About AgriCare

A farm app that helps Suarez Farm care for its crops.

Why we built it

Spotting a leaf disease early, knowing when the soil needs water or fertilizer, and planning around the weather are everyday worries on a farm. AgriCare was made for Suarez Farm in Pandi, Bulacan, to make each of them easier, right on an Android phone.

A farmer takes a photo of a leaf, and an AI says whether it is healthy or sick, and what to do. Sensors in each field report the soil's moisture and its nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium, so the app can say when the soil is dry or needs fertilizer. Weather advice comes from this week's forecast for Pandi, and alerts keep everyone up to date, in English or Filipino.

There are three apps: Farmer, for the people who work the fields; Admin, for the farm Owner, who follows up diseases, watches the sensors and manages farmer accounts; and Developer, for the AgriCare team, who answer questions and send announcements. AgriCare is a capstone project of BSIT students at STI College San Jose Del Monte.
