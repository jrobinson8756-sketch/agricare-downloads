About AgriCare

A phone app and soil sensors that help Suarez Farm catch crop diseases early and know what its soil needs.

Why we built it

Farming is the main livelihood in much of Central Luzon. At Suarez Farm in Pandi, Bulacan, the owner grows rice on about 1.7 hectares, with tomatoes, eggplant, chili and watermelon on the side. As on most farms nearby, the crops were checked by eye and by experience. That works, but it takes time, and a disease is easy to miss until it spreads.

The soil was harder still. Some parts are clay and some are rocky, and crops grow differently in each, but there was no way to measure it. So fertilizer was a guess: too much makes rice grow lush but bear little grain, and too little holds it back.

When we interviewed the owner, a farmer for over 20 years, the request was clear: one simple app, in Tagalog, for the soil, the crops' health and the weather. Almost every farmer there has a phone and internet, so a phone app made sense.

What AgriCare does

  1. Scan a leaf. Take a photo of a rice, tomato, eggplant or chili leaf. The AI says whether it is healthy or which disease it may have, and what to do, in English or Filipino.
  2. Check the soil. A sensor in each field sends the soil's moisture and its nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium (NPK). The app then says when the soil is dry or low in nutrients, and which fertilizer to add.
  3. Plan around the weather. This week's forecast for Pandi becomes advice on when to water, when to hold off on fertilizer, and how to get ready for heavy rain, heat or strong wind.
  4. Get alerts. The phone tells you when a scan finds a disease, the soil needs care, or bad weather is coming.

Who it's for

There are three apps, one for each kind of user:

  • Farmer, for the people who work the fields.
  • Admin, for the owner, who follows up diseases, reads the sensor history and makes farmer accounts.
  • Developer, for our team, who answer help requests and send announcements.

Our capstone

AgriCare is our capstone project for the BS in Information Technology at STI College San Jose Del Monte. Its paper is titled:

AgriCare: An IoT-Based Mobile System for Crop Health Monitoring Using Image Recognition and Soil Quality Analysis at Suarez Farm

We built it to cut crop losses, use water and fertilizer wisely, and give other farms nearby a model to follow.
